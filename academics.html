Dual-Track Curricular Excellence

Synthesizing Divine Guidance with Scientific Mastery

Our curriculum is carefully architected so students never have to compromise between deep Quranic fluency and top-tier achievement in mathematics, science, English, and computing.

📖
Spiritual Pillar

The Quranic Program

Every student from early childhood to high school graduates receives daily structured Quranic instruction under qualified Qaris and scholars.

1. Nazra-e-Quran & Tajweed:

Flawless Makharij pronunciation, rules of Noon Sakinah, Meem Sakinah, and rhythm recitation.

2. Dedicated Hifz Track:

Full-time or harmonized memorization with dedicated daily revision (Sabaq, Sabqi, Manzil) protocols.

3. Tafseer, Hadith & Fiqh:

Practical understanding of Islamic rulings, biographies of the Masoomeen (AS), and daily life ethics.

🔬
Academic Pillar

Modern Sciences & Arts

Aligned with international benchmarks and Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Rawalpindi standards.

1. Mathematics & Analytical Logic:

Arithmetic foundations advancing to geometry, algebra, trigonometry, and analytical calculus.

2. Laboratory Sciences:

Hands-on demonstrations in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories led by senior specialists.

3. English & Computer Science:

Bilingual fluency, creative writing, public discourse, computer coding, and information technology.
