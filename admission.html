Step-by-Step

Our 4-Stage Admission Roadmap

A welcoming, transparent, and supportive enrollment journey for parents and students.

1

Application Submission

Fill out the online application below or visit our Balkasar campus to obtain the registration docket.

2

Diagnostic Evaluation

An encouraging, age-appropriate assessment in basic English, Math, and Nazra Quran to ascertain student readiness.

3

Parent & Student Meeting

A friendly conversation with our Principal and Academic Head to align on values, mutual expectations, and goals.

4

Enrollment Confirmation

Submission of required documents, fee voucher settlement, and welcoming the student into the HES family.

Required Paperwork

Documents Checklist

Please bring original documents and copies at the time of your interview:

  • Nadra B-Form / Birth Certificate: Copy of the student's official identification.
  • Parent/Guardian CNIC: 2 clear photocopies of Father / Guardian National Identity Card.
  • Passport-Size Photographs: 4 recent passport-size photographs with blue/white background.
  • Previous School Leaving Certificate (SLC): Required for admission into Grade 1 and above.
  • Previous Academic Report Card: Last examination results from previous institution.
