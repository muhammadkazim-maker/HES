FACILITIES IN DETAIL

Campus Infrastructure & Amenities

Every department and facility is engineered for student safety, modern scholarship, and moral cultivation.

Smart Ventilated Classrooms

Low student-to-teacher ratio (average 22 students per section) ensuring personalized attention, continuous formative assessment, and comfortable ergonomic desks with natural airflow.

Islamic & Academic Library

Over 5,000 reference volumes spanning Quranic Tafseer, authentic Hadith collections, children's storybooks, and competitive examination sets in a quiet, curated reading hall.

Modern Science Laboratories

Equipped with optical microscopes, electronic balances, chemical fume areas, and board-certified experimental rigs to bring textbook theories to vibrant life through hands-on practice.

Computer & Digital Lab

Individual workstations empowering students to learn touch-typing, computer programming basics, digital document publishing, and online research ethics via optical fiber internet.

Central Mosque & Hifz Sanctuary

A sacred, calm spiritual heart of the campus. Students participate in congregational Zuhr prayer, learning wudu etiquette and respectful mosque manners firsthand under guided faculty supervision.

Sports Grounds & Athletics

Physical wellness is essential to sound scholarship. Our green campus grounds host seasonal cricket leagues, football matches, badminton, and structured morning physical conditioning.

Dedicated School Transport

Reliable pick-and-drop service operated by background-verified drivers with scheduled routes across Balkasar, Pipli, and surrounding rural and city centers.

Hygienic Water & Canteen

Equipped with reverse-osmosis multi-stage water filtration ensuring clean drinking water across all student blocks, plus healthy snack options strictly inspected for hygiene.

24/7 Security & Health Station

Complete boundary wall security with stationed guards, CCTV camera monitoring across all public corridors, and equipped first aid room for medical emergencies.
