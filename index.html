About Our Institution

A Sacred Mission of Wisdom, Faith & Modern Discovery

Welcome to Hadi Education System, a premier educational sanctum where learning meets character building, and students are empowered to achieve their highest worldly and spiritual potential. Located in the heart of Chakwal at Pipli Road, Balkasar, our school is dedicated to cultivating well-rounded personalities.

Founded in 2005, we have spent over two decades pioneering a dual-track model where children memorize the Holy Quran with authentic Tajweed while mastering Physics, Mathematics, English, and Computing without compromise.

Learning Pathway

The Student's Journey at HES

Structured growth stages designed to nurture curious minds from early childhood to high school graduation.

01
Nursery to KG

Early Years

Play-based discovery, Arabic Noorani Qaida phonetics, basic numeracy, English letter tracing, and joyful Islamic manners.

02
Classes 1 to 5

Primary School

Complete Nazra recitation, foundational mathematics, English reading fluency, science curiosity, and Urdu literature.

03
Classes 6 to 8

Middle School

Optional accelerated Hifz track, computer lab programming, algebra, practical science experiments, and critical thinking.

04
Classes 9, 10 & ICS

Matric & High School

Rigorous Rawalpindi Board exam coaching in Science / Computer Science groups with career counseling and leadership ethics.
