About Our Institution

Hear from Our Principal

Welcome to Hadi Education System, a premier educational sanctum where learning meets character building, and students are empowered to achieve their highest worldly and spiritual potential. Located in the heart of Chakwal at Pipli Road, Balkasar, our school is dedicated to cultivating well-rounded personalities.

Since 2017, we have been developing a dual-track model where children learn the Holy Quran with authentic Tajweed while building a strong foundation in science, mathematics, English, and computing.

Muhammad Ali Hamdani Principal, Hadi Education System

Learning Pathway

The Student's Journey at HES

As an institution registered with the Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (FBISE), HES offers learning from Classes 6 to 12, including ICS, Pre-Medical, and Pre-Engineering.

01
Classes 6 to 8

Middle School

Optional accelerated Hifz track, computer lab programming, algebra, practical science experiments, and critical thinking.

02
Classes 9, 10 & ICS

Matric & High School

Focused FBISE examination preparation in Science and Computer Science, complemented by career guidance and leadership development.

03
Quranic & Islamic Studies

Quranic Education & Ahkam

Quranic learning nurtures recitation, understanding, and reflection. Students also explore essential Ahkam and Islamic manners, connecting their learning with everyday life.
